Starting

Your book will appear here, page by page, the way an e-reader shows it.

Every step runs on this device. Nothing is uploaded, so private and large documents are fine.

  1. 1
    Add a PDF. Title, author and bookmarks are read from the file.
  2. 2
    Convert. Lines are rejoined into paragraphs, hyphenated words are repaired, headers and page numbers are removed, and figures are cut out of the page.
  3. 3
    Check the proof, then download. The EPUB 3 file opens in Apple Books, Kobo, Google Play Books, Calibre, and Kindle via Send to Kindle.

What converts well

Novels, reports, papers and manuals with a real text layer convert best. Scanned PDFs are read with text recognition (OCR), which is very good on clean print but can misread faded or handwritten pages; Page images keeps them exactly as they look. Tables and vector charts are kept as text lines, and complex multi-column magazine layouts may need the Page images layout.
